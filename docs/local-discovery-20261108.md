# FitGoIn: local discovery with Geoapify

Local discovery is an authenticated, opt-in search for gyms, sports stores and grocery stores within 15 km of a user-entered city centre. The application does not request phone GPS or transmit profile, health, or conversation data to Geoapify. Distances are independently calculated and results outside the radius are discarded. City ambiguity requires clarification. Addresses come from Geoapify / OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete or outdated; prices, stock, and gym fees are not verified.

The key is server-side only. Store it in Supabase Edge Functions secrets as Geoapify (case-sensitive), or use GEOAPIFY_API_KEY. A stored key opts into lookup when the switch is absent; FGI_LOCAL_GEO_ENABLED=false always disables it. Never put the key in the repository or frontend. User access and fgi_local_claim quota checks run before outbound requests: 4 searches per user per UTC day and 100 total per UTC month. Each accepted search makes at most one geocoding request and one Places request, with a maximum of 20 places. The frontend shows the provider and source attribution, including links to Geoapify and OpenStreetMap.

Before enabling: verify the account's plan, key restrictions and quotas; test a signed-in search and disabled/consent/quota paths. Search does not provide store product prices or membership costs. Do not report this integration live until the key is configured, the backend and UI are deployed, and a real authenticated search succeeds.
